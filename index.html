Song Wen

I am currently a Research Scientist at Meta. My research spans Generative AI, Multimodal Large Language Models, Neural ODEs, and Computer Vision, with a focus on model development, training, and data pipelines.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Rutgers University, where I was advised by Prof. Dimitris N. Metaxas. Prior to that, I obtained my M.Phil. degree from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) and my B.Eng. degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST).

Research Interests

Generative AI

Diffusion models, flow matching, rectified flows, controllable generation, image synthesis and editing

Multimodal LLMs

Vision-language understanding, multimodal instruction tuning, omni-modal modeling

Neural ODEs

Trajectory forecasting, multi-agent interaction modeling, dynamical systems

Computer Vision

Efficient neural architectures, medical image segmentation, video super-resolution
