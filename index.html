Song Wen

I am currently a Research Scientist at Meta. My research interests lie in Generative AI, Diffusion Models, Multimodal Large Language Models, Neural ODEs, and Computer Vision, with a focus on developing principled models and data pipelines.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Rutgers University, where I was advised by Prof. Dimitris N. Metaxas. Prior to that, I obtained my M.Phil. degree from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) and my B.Eng. degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST).
