Song Wen

Research Scientist at Meta

I am a Research Scientist at Meta. My research focuses on Generative AI, Diffusion Models, Multimodal Large Language Models, Neural ODEs, and Computer Vision.

I received my Ph.D. from Rutgers University, advised by Prof. Dimitris N. Metaxas. I also hold a degree from HKUST, where I worked with Prof. S.-H. Gary Chan.
